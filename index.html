Help & support

      
    

    
FAQ

    
How do I share my card?
Tap "Share my card" on the home screen, or open a card and use the Share button. The other person gets your contact details instantly.

    
How do invites work?
Share your invite code from Dashboard → Invite friends. Your friend gets 7 days of Pro free, and you unlock premium designs: 7 invites for Midnight, 15 for Gold Foil, 30 for Neon.

    
How do I get premium designs?
Buy them directly under Dashboard → Premium designs (Midnight $0.99, Gold Foil $1.99, Neon $2.99), invite friends to unlock them free, or get them all included with Pro or Premium.

    
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything is encrypted on your phone with your PIN before it's stored or synced. We can't read your data.

Privacy Policy

    
Last updated: October 11, 2026

    
What we collect: OMNI encrypts all your card data on your device with your PIN before storage or sync. We never see your PIN, your cards, or your contacts. When you use Cloud Sync, we store only the encrypted blob — we cannot decrypt it.

    
Analytics: We collect anonymous aggregate counts (total users, cards created) via our stats service. No personal identifiers are attached. If you use a referral code, we store the code mapping to award design unlocks — this is the only account-linked data we hold.

    
Data retention: Your encrypted data stays on our servers until you delete your account or disable Cloud Sync. Anonymous analytics are retained indefinitely in aggregate form.

    
Your rights (GDPR/CCPA): You can request your data anytime. You can delete your account and all cloud data from Data controls → Delete account. Contact us at hello@omni-tap.com for data requests.
