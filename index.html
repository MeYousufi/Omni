    Help & support


    
FAQ

    
How do I share my card?
Tap "Share my card" on the home screen, or open a card and use the Share button. The other person gets your contact details instantly.

    
How do invites work?
Share your invite code from Dashboard → Sharing. Your friend gets 7 days of Pro free, and you earn 100 tokens per friend.

    
What are tokens for?
Tokens unlock premium card designs (Aurora, Obsidian Gold, Royale) under Dashboard → Sharing → Premium designs.

    
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything is encrypted on your phone with your PIN before it's stored or synced. We can't read your data.

Privacy Policy

    
Last updated: October 11, 2026

    
What we collect: OMNI encrypts all your card data on your device with your PIN before storage or sync. We never see your PIN, your cards, or your contacts. When you use Cloud Sync, we store only the encrypted blob — we cannot decrypt it.

    
Analytics: We collect anonymous aggregate counts (total users, cards created) via our stats service. No personal identifiers are attached. If you use a referral code, we store the code mapping to award tokens — this is the only account-linked data we hold.

    
Data retention: Your encrypted data stays on our servers until you delete your account or disable Cloud Sync. Anonymous analytics are retained indefinitely in aggregate form.

    
Your rights (GDPR/CCPA): You can export your data anytime (Back up → Download). You can delete your account and all cloud data from Data controls → Delete account. Contact us at hello@omni-tap.com for data requests.
