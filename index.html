001 / THE PROBLEM

Paper cards die in wallets.

You print five hundred. You hand out forty. The rest gather dust in a drawer — and the forty you gave out go stale the moment your number changes.

Every meeting ends the same way: a card that leads nowhere.

002 / WHY IT HAPPENS

The moment is too fast for paper.

You get about three seconds when you meet someone. That's not enough time to dig through a wallet, find a card, and hope it's still current — or to dictate a number into a phone while trying to make eye contact.

So the exchange never quite happens, and the connection is lost.

003 / MEET OMNI

One card. Always up to date.

Update your number once and every card you've ever shared reflects it instantly. No reprints. No stale info. No wallet required.

You choose exactly what each person sees — share everything with a client, just a name with a stranger.

004 / BUILT ON TRUST

Your contacts stay yours.

Your cards are encrypted on your device with a PIN only you know. We can't read them, sell them, or lose them in a breach — because we never have them.
