OMNI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What we collect

OMNI encrypts all your card data on your device with your PIN before storage or sync. We never see your PIN, your cards, or your contacts. When you use Cloud Sync, we store only the encrypted blob — we cannot decrypt it.

Analytics

We collect anonymous aggregate counts (total users, cards created) via our stats service. No personal identifiers are attached. If you use a referral code, we store the code mapping to award tokens — this is the only account-linked data we hold.

Data retention

Your encrypted data stays on our servers until you delete your account or disable Cloud Sync. Anonymous analytics are retained indefinitely in aggregate form.

Your rights (GDPR/CCPA)

You have the right to access, export, correct, and delete your data. Export anytime via Back up → Download. Delete your account and all cloud data via Data controls → Delete account. We respond to requests within 30 days.

Children

OMNI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes means you accept them.
